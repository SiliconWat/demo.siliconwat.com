siliconwat.com: drawings

siliconwat.com will be a free finder from Silicon Wat℠ for the Khmer Tipiṭaka: a person asks in their own words, in Khmer or English, and sees the typed pages of the Buddhist Institute edition (1927–69) that may answer, with the lines highlighted. It writes no answers of its own; every passage drawn here is real, as typed by Se Mong Ly, and the questions are made up. Every screen is drawn on a phone in English and Khmer, with two desktop screens and a dark pass.

The icon is a placeholder: Silicon Wat's own mark is not settled.
