About the finder

[ PLACEHOLDER ICON ] Silicon Wat's own mark is not settled

What it does

You ask in your own words, in Khmer or English. It shows the typed pages of the Khmer Tipiṭaka that may answer, with the lines highlighted, and where each one is: basket, volume and page.

What it does not do

It writes no answers and no summaries. Every word on a result is the Tipiṭaka as typed. To talk a passage over, Ask an AI copies it for you, and you choose where.

How everyday words find a page

Behind the search, a computer wrote down once, in Khmer and English, the questions each page may answer, so that a question in everyday words can find it. Those questions are never shown to anyone.

Your privacy

Your question is searched on this device, and your questions are kept only on it. Visits are counted without cookies, and the count never sees a question.

So far 36,211 Khmer pages and 13,921 Pāli pages of the edition are typed. The typing continues.
